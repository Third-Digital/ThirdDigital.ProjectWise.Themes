Windows Terminal colour scheme data
==================================

The nine preset colour schemes are transcribed from Microsoft Windows Terminal:
https://github.com/microsoft/terminal/blob/1282d56a33bb15206fcb3a09d3bea0a33036f2cd/src/cascadia/TerminalSettingsModel/defaults.json
Revision: 1282d56a33bb15206fcb3a09d3bea0a33036f2cd
The palette data has been adapted to the module's C++ ColourScheme structure.
